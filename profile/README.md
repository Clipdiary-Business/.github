# Clipdiary Business

> Clipdiary Business keeps yesterday's paste on one Windows desk: open the clip journal, search the history, and leave saved clips on the same office clipboard.

## Four Beats

1. **Name the journal.** Give the Clipdiary Business office clipboard a short title before you hunt through yesterday.
2. **Pin the live trail.** Keep current pastes on Clipdiary Business clip journal and leave unused days aside.
3. **Search once.** Open Clipdiary Business search history before you archive a saved clip.
4. **Close the loop.** Clear finished pastes so the next Clipdiary Business visit does not start with a search.

## First Questions

**Does Clipdiary Business replace every other program?**
No. Clipdiary Business sits beside the editor, browser, and files you already use.

**Can two people share one computer?**
Yes. Each person keeps a separate Clipdiary Business profile.

**What should a new user try first?**
One small Clipdiary Business search today, then the same saved clips tomorrow.

**Does search history leave the office clipboard?**
No. Clipdiary Business search history stays with Clipdiary Business saved clips on that journal.

## Who Sits Down at Clipdiary Business

- **Journal** — the trail stays on Clipdiary Business clip journal.
- **Search** — queries stay on Clipdiary Business search history.
- **Saved clips** — keepers stay on Clipdiary Business saved clips.
- **Office clipboard** — the name stays on one Clipdiary Business desk.
- **Shared desk** — each Clipdiary Business profile keeps its own pins.
- **Handoff** — the next person can read the Clipdiary Business note without a tour.

## Field Note

Clipdiary Business is easier to trust when the office clipboard stays smaller than the folder it came from. A named journal shows what is active, the search pins show what you will find today, and the closing note tells the next person which saved clip changed.

## One Real Pass
